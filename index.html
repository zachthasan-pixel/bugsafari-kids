Frequently Asked Questions

What age is Bug Safari Kids designed for?
Bug Safari Kids is designed for children ages 4-8. The game uses simple tap interactions, large friendly text, and encouraging audio narration so young children can play independently.

Does the app require an internet connection?
No. Bug Safari Kids works entirely offline after download.

Does the app contain ads or in-app purchases?
No. Bug Safari Kids is a one-time purchase with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions.

Does the app collect personal data?
No. Bug Safari Kids does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Bug Safari Kids is developed by Zach Hassan. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

Bug Safari Kids does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App does not:

  • Collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Track location data
  • Use cookies or advertising identifiers
  • Connect to the internet during gameplay
  • Include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Bug Safari Kids is designed for children and is fully compliant with COPPA. The App does not collect any personal information from any users, including children under 13.

Game Progress Data

The App saves game progress locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.
